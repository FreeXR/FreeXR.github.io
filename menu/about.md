FreeXR is a project that plans to port linux, openXR/[stardustxr](https://stardustxr.org/), and the full linux userspace to VR headsets like the Quest 3/3s. The plan is to fully port over postmarketOS or some other linux distrubution, such as debian, but first we need to replace the kernel, and get a linux userspace (requring porting of drivers).
